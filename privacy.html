# Privacy Policy — CatDoku: Find Cat CatDoku: Find Cat is a puzzle game by Somia Elkhoussi. The app does not require an account. It uses Google AdMob for advertisements and Google Analytics for Firebase to understand gameplay and app usage. ## Data handled by the app and its providers Our advertising and analytics providers may collect: - Device and app identifiers, including an app-instance identifier and, when iOS tracking permission allows, the advertising identifier (IDFA). - Device information such as model, operating system, language and app version. - Approximate location derived from network information such as an IP address. The app does not request precise GPS location. - Usage and gameplay events, including level starts, completion, retries, scores, remaining paws and rewards. - Advertising information, such as ads displayed and interactions with those ads. - Crash, performance and other diagnostic information collected by the advertising SDK. These providers use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, provide usage analytics and evaluate SDK performance. Some information is associated with device or app identifiers, even though the game does not ask you to register an account. ## Advertising and tracking choices The app requests tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt when available. If you allow tracking, the advertising provider may use the IDFA for personalized advertising and advertising measurement across apps and websites. You can review or change this permission in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Declining tracking does not prevent you from playing. The app may still display ads and collect data needed for analytics and advertising that does not require tracking permission. A tracking choice is not a request to delete previously collected information. ## Local game progress Level progress, scores, streaks and preferences are saved locally on your device. Separately, the app sends selected gameplay events and scores to Firebase Analytics as described above. Removing the app does not automatically delete information already held by service providers. ## Service providers - Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads - Google Analytics for Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy The app also retrieves its advertising configuration from a GitHub-hosted file. This network request is subject to GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement ## Retention and privacy requests Local data remains subject to the app's storage and the device's backup settings. Data held by service providers is subject to the applicable service settings and their retention policies. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to certain processing. Contact us for help with a request or a question about the app's data practices. ## Contact Email: contact@puzzlestime.com Website: https://puzzlestime.com ## Changes We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and show the effective date on the published policy.
